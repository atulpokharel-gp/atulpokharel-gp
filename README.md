## 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Forward-Deployed AI Security Engineer** working at the intersection of **agentic AI**, **AI security**, and **real-world delivery**. 8 years turning ambiguous problems into deployed, reliable, **secure** systems — from customer discovery and rapid prototyping to production.

I like building AI that is not just powerful, but **secure and trustworthy**:

- **AI security** — securing agents, connectors, credentials, and the plaintext secrets they leave behind
- Agentic systems that reason, call tools, and execute workflows behind **guardrails and approval gates**
- Deterministic **verification** of AI output (no blind trust in the model)
- Reverse engineering & binary analysis for AI-generated **security findings**
- Turning scattered knowledge and complex software into usable, **verifiable** intelligence

> **Builder philosophy:**
> **AI should explain clearly, act safely, and prove what it claims.**

🎓 M.S. in Computer Science — **AI, AI Agents & Cybersecurity**

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🛡️ [Zinloop](https://zinloop.com/) — AI-connector security for MSPs
> *Sole technical founder.* **AI security** for the agent era.

Discovers the AI assistants and connectors running on client machines and secures the plaintext secrets they leave behind. Includes **Zipax**, a developer-security profiler that reads AI coding sessions and scores how safely a developer works with agents, MCP, and credentials.

`Python` · `AI/connector security` · `Secrets discovery` · `MCP` · `Agentic analysis`

### 🔎 [groundre](https://grounder.zinloop.com/) — deterministic verification for AI security reports
> *Solo founder.* "An AI agent may propose claims — deterministic verifiers dispose."

Re-derives every claim in an AI-generated reverse-engineering / **vulnerability report** from the actual binary using **Ghidra (PyGhidra)** and **radare2**, returning **CONFIRMED / CONTRADICTED / UNVERIFIED** verdicts with evidence, plus a PASS / REVIEW / FAIL **security release gate**. Ships with a provider-agnostic agent layer, an MCP server, and a GitHub Action.

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `Ghidra` · `radare2` · `Reverse engineering` · `MCP` · `GitHub Actions` · `Docker`

### 🧬 [Cytible](https://cytible.com/) — AI evidence intelligence for biomed & biotech investing
> *Co-founder & technical lead.* Scientific answers you can trace back to the source — built with the same verify-everything **security discipline**.

Searches integrated biomedical databases (PubMed/NCBI, Europe PMC, Semantic Scholar, ClinicalTrials.gov, UniProt, ChEMBL, Open Targets, RCSB PDB, SEC EDGAR, NIH RePORTER) and classifies each piece of evidence **SUPPORTS / WEAKENS / CONTRADICTS** — with a Claim Ledger, evidence chain-of-custody, reproducibility & decision-readiness scoring, and investor/researcher reports.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `FastAPI` · `Postgres + pgvector (Neon)` · `Redis` · `Claude` · `Docker / AWS`
